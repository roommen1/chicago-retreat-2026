O'Hare (ORD) ↔ Hotel EMC2

Bags: carry-on only. Airport: Chicago O'Hare (ORD). United typically lands at Terminal 1, American at Terminal 3 — terminals are connected by the free ATS train, or it's a brisk 10-minute walk.

Individuals listed in bold with an asterisk (*) order the rideshare for their group. Charge it to your Ramp Retreat Travel Stipend card (add it to Apple/Google Pay ahead of time). If Ramp doesn't work, use your own card and send your receipt to [Name] for reimbursement.

How to order a rideshare

1. Open Uber or Lyft
Whichever app you already have works fine.
2. Enter the destination
Type "Hotel EMC2, Chicago" and select it.
3. Pick the right size
3+ people: choose UberXL/Lyft XL. Otherwise please order a standard economy ride.
4. Match & go
Confirm the plate and driver photo. Charge your Ramp card, save the receipt, and add a memo.

From Ogilvie to Hotel

STEP 1 — Take Metra to Ogilvie
Board your suburb's Union Pacific line heading downtown. Buy your ticket via the Ventra app before boarding, or from a station agent/machine.
STEP 2 — Ogilvie to Hotel EMC2
Hotel EMC2 is about 1.5–2 miles from Ogilvie. A short rideshare (10–15 min) or a CTA bus/L connection both work well; use the RTA Trip Planner for the current best route.
STEP 3 — Same route home
Reverse the trip when you head home — check Metra's schedule for return times, since suburban service can run less frequently in the evening.
